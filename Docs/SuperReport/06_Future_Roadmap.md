# 06: Future Roadmap - Version 2.0

With the successful launch of MVP 1.0, the foundation is laid for future expansion into a more comprehensive healthcare platform.

## 1. Professional Onboarding & Verification
- **Medical License OCR:** Implementing automated OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to verify doctor licenses instantly.
- **Admin Approval App:** A mobile interface for administrators to approve/deny new medical staff registrations on the go.

## 2. Advanced Clinical Tools
- **Prescription System:** Integration with pharmacy networks to send electronic prescriptions directly to patients' nearest pharmacies.
- **AI Triage Assistant:** Using LLMs (Large Language Models) to assist doctors by summarizing patient symptoms and suggesting preliminary classifications based on the PHQ-9.

## 3. Monetization & Growth
- **Viva Coins Wallet:** A secure, in-app wallet for seamless payments using Stripe and Omise.
- **Subscription Model (Viva Care):** A monthly subscription providing unlimited Clubhouse access and discounted telemed consultations.
- **Referral Rewards:** Incentivizing users to grow the community while maintaining anonymity.

## 4. Enhanced Community Interaction
- **Screen Sharing:** Allowing doctors to share therapeutic visual aids during video calls.
- **Collaborative Whiteboards:** Real-time drawing tools for group therapy sessions in the Clubhouse zone.
- **Multimedia Chat:** Support for sharing voice notes, documents, and images within secure consultation channels.
